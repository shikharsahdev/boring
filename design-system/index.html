A few things you might wonder

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Let’s talk it through ↗
Do my parents need a smartphone?

No. The experience is designed around phone calls and visits at home. The family app is there for those who want to use it.

Can I coordinate care from another country?

That’s who we’re designing for, too. You can follow appointments and updates, while your parents get care at home in India.

Will a doctor explain the results?

Yes. A doctor’s review is part of each plan, with clear explanations and guidance on what to do next.

How do I know which plan is right?

Start with a conversation about your family, where they live, and the care they need. The team can explain what is included and check availability.

Does this replace my parents’ regular doctor?

Boring is intended to support ongoing preventive care. Your parents can continue seeing their regular doctor. It is not an emergency service.
