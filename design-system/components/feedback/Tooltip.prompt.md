Explains a medical term in plain words. Opens on hover and on tap, because half the audience is on a phone.

```jsx
<Tooltip content="HbA1c shows your average blood sugar over about three months.">
  <span style={{ borderBottom: "1px dashed var(--border-strong)" }}>HbA1c</span>
</Tooltip>
```

Never hide anything necessary behind a tooltip — parents may not find it.
